# Business and asset sources

Researched September 8, 2026. This is an independent educational concept for an existing Philippine business. The brand is not the student's invention, and the business has not endorsed this project.

## Business information

- [Official Yardstick website](https://yardstickcoffee.com/): business identity and official destination.
- [Subscription collection](https://store.yardstickcoffee.com/collections/subscriptions): Golden Ticket from ₱1,250, Legazpi from ₱1,350, and Home Brew ₱1,650.
- [Subscription FAQ](https://store.yardstickcoffee.com/pages/subscription-faqs): package sizes, monthly subscriptions, skip/cancel links, gifting, and Coffee Club membership.
- [General FAQ](https://store.yardstickcoffee.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions): grind options, `hello@yardstickcoffee.com`, and `sales@yardstickcoffee.com`.
- [Golden Ticket subscription](https://store.yardstickcoffee.com/products/monthly-subscription-golden-ticket-250g-x-2-bags-or-1kg-x-1-bag): featured product, flavor notes, and actual purchase destination.
- [Legazpi subscription](https://store.yardstickcoffee.com/products/monthly-subscription-legazpi-250g-x-2-bags-or-1kg-x1-bag): featured plan and product photo.

Only the two-bag versions are priced on this concept. Larger bag prices differed between the FAQ and live product data, so they were not advertised. Shipping thresholds and dispatch wording also differed across official pages; users are directed to the official checkout for current details. No made-up discount, free trial, shipping guarantee, review score, or customer count is presented.
